## Open the Reactions page

<Steps>

1. In the Admin CP, click **Configuration**, then **Reactions** in the menu on the left.

   <Image src={menu} alt="The Configuration menu with Reactions circled" />

</Steps>

The page lists every reaction in the order members see them, with how many times each has
been used.

## Add a reaction

<Steps>

1. Click **Add Reaction**.
2. Type a **Name** - up to 50 characters - and choose an **Image**, then click
   **Save Reaction**.

   <Image src={add} alt="The Add Reaction form with the name Fire and an image chosen" />

</Steps>

The image has to be a PNG, GIF, JPEG, WebP or SVG of up to 256 KB. Square images look best:
they're shown small (18 pixels) in a post's summary and bigger (36) in the pop-up.

<Aside>
  An SVG can carry code that would run on the forum for anyone who opened the image, so
  the forum cleans every SVG you upload: it keeps the drawing and removes scripts, links
  and anything else that could run or fetch something. If it removed anything, it says so
  when you save. An ordinary icon comes through unchanged.
</Aside>

## Edit a reaction

Click its name. You can change the name, the order, and whether it's active, and upload a
new image - or leave the image empty to keep the one it has. Members who reacted with it
keep their reaction.

## Change the order

<Steps>

1. Change the numbers in the **Order** column - lowest first - and click **Save Order**.

   <Image src={order} alt="The Order column, with Save Order circled" />

</Steps>

The first active reaction is the forum's **default**: the one members get when they just
click the button. It's marked "(default)" in the list.

## Hide a reaction

<Steps>

1. Click **Options** beside it, then **Deactivate**.

   <Image src={options} alt="The Options menu beside Angry, with Deactivate circled" />

</Steps>

A deactivated reaction disappears from the pop-up and from posts' summaries. Nobody's
reaction is lost, though: activate it again (**Options**, then **Activate**) and they all
come back.

## Delete a reaction

Click **Options**, then **Delete**. You're asked first, and told how many times it has been
used - **every one of those reactions is deleted with it**.

<Image src={confirm} alt="The delete confirmation, saying how many times the reaction has been used" />

<Aside type="tip">
  If you might want them back, deactivate the reaction instead.
</Aside>

Every change is recorded in the administrator log (**Tools & Maintenance**, then
**Administrator Log**).
