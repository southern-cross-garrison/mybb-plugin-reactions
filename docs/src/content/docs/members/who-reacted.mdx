Under every post that has reactions is a summary: the post's most used reactions, and how
many there are. If one of them is yours it says "You and 3 others".

## See who reacted

<Steps>

1. Click the summary under the post.

   <Image src={summary} alt="A post's reaction summary, circled" />

2. Everyone who reacted is listed, newest first. Click a reaction's tab to see just the
   people who picked it, or a name to open their profile.

   <Image src={who} alt="The list of who reacted, with tabs for All, Love, Like and Haha" />

</Steps>
