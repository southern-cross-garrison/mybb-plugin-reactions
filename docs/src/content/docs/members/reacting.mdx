You can react to any post you can read, including your own and posts in closed threads.
You have one reaction per post.

## Like a post

<Steps>

1. Click **Like** under the post.

   <Image src={button} alt="A post's reactions, with the Like button circled" />

</Steps>

The button turns blue and shows your reaction, and the post's summary now counts you.

## Pick a different reaction

<Steps>

1. Rest your mouse on **Like** under the post, without clicking. After a moment the
   reactions pop up above it.

   <Image src={picker} alt="The reactions popped up above the Like button: Like, Love, Haha, Wow, Sad and Angry" />

2. Click the reaction you want.

   <Image src={reacted} alt="The post with Love picked: the button reads Love and the summary reads You and 4 others" />

</Steps>

On a phone or tablet, **press and hold** the button instead, then tap the reaction.

<Image src={pickerPhone} alt="The reactions popped up above the button on a phone" />

<Aside type="tip">
  A quick tap or click is always the plain reaction - Like, or taking yours back. Hold, or
  rest the mouse, to choose.
</Aside>

## Change your reaction

Pick a different reaction the same way, and it replaces your old one. You never have more
than one reaction on a post.

## Take your reaction back

Click your reaction under the post - the blue button - and it's gone. Picking the same
reaction again from the pop-up does the same.

## Using the keyboard

Tab to the button and press **Enter** to like the post (or take your reaction back). Press
the **up arrow** to open the reactions, the **left** and **right arrows** to move between
them, and **Enter** to pick one. **Escape** closes them.
