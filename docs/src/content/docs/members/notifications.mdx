The first time someone reacts to one of your posts, you get a private message from them
saying how they reacted, with a link to the post.

<Image src={message} alt="A private message: trooper1 reacted to your post in Reactions Test Thread with: Like" />

You hear about each person once per post. If they change their reaction, or take it back
and react again, you don't get another message. You're never told about your own
reactions.

## Turn reaction messages off

<Steps>

1. Go to **User CP**, then **Edit Options**.
2. Under the private messaging options, untick **Send me a private message when someone
   reacts to my posts**, and save.

   <Image src={option} alt="The User CP options, with the reaction notification checkbox circled" />

</Steps>

You also won't get them if you've turned private messages off, or put the person on your
ignore list.
