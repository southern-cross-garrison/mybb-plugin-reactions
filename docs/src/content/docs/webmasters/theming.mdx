Activating the plugin edits two of MyBB's templates in every theme:

- `postbit` and `postbit_classic` get `{$post['reactions']}` straight after
  `{$post['signature']}` - under the message, outside the part quick edit replaces.
- `usercp_options_pms` gets `{$reactions_usercp_option}` at the end.

Deactivating takes both out again. If you add a theme after activating, deactivate and
activate the plugin to edit that theme too. A theme without `{$post['signature']}` in its
postbit won't get the bar; add `{$post['reactions']}` wherever you want it.

## Restyling

The plugin's look is in the `reactions.css` stylesheet, attached to the master theme so
every theme inherits it. To change it for one theme, edit that theme's copy under
**Templates & Style**, then **Themes**. Colours use Bootstrap's `--bs-body-bg` and
`--bs-primary` where a theme defines them.

The plugin's templates are in the **Reactions** group under **Templates & Style**, then
**Templates**.
