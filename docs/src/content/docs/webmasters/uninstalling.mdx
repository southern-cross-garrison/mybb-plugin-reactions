**Deactivating** (Configuration, then Plugins) takes the reactions bar out of posts and
keeps everything else. Activating again brings it all back.

**Uninstalling** removes the plugin's tables, settings, templates, stylesheet and the images
uploaded to `uploads/reactions/`, and the column it adds to the users table.

<Aside type="caution">
  Uninstalling deletes every reaction ever made, and they can't be brought back.
</Aside>

After uninstalling, delete the plugin's files: `inc/plugins/reactions.php`,
`inc/plugins/reactions/`, `admin/modules/config/reactions.php`,
`inc/languages/english/reactions.lang.php`,
`inc/languages/english/admin/config_reactions.lang.php`, `images/reactions/` and
`jscripts/reactions/`.
